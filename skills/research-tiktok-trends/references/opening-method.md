# Opening mechanisms in trend research

Use this reference when the synthesis needs an opening mechanism or a practical opening
recommendation. Use the evidence and output fields the workflow already selected. This reference
adds no discovery, ranking, watching or model stage.

## Decide what the current evidence supports

Titles, captions and untimed transcripts can suggest a topic or writing direction. They cannot
establish the actual first frame, on-screen text timing, first sound, delivery or audiovisual
alignment. Describe an unobserved opening as a proposed production choice, not a finding about
what that video did. For watched evidence, keep picture, heard sound and their joint effect
distinct; a transcript is not a listening observation. Keep existing content citations attached
to observed claims. Do not infer private retention from public plays or a reference's success.

## Choose a mechanism that the creator can make

The opening patterns are read only through the script, never from the files under
`references/hook-core/`: `rank` prints the catalog (each pattern's id, name and material
requirements), and `cd '<run directory>' && KABO_STATIC_RUN_DIR='<run directory>' python3 '<skill directory>/scripts/prepare_hook_context.py' --platform tiktok
--purpose trends --pattern-id <Mxx> --output hook-opening-<Mxx>.json` prints one to three chosen
patterns in full: picture, sound, intended joint effect, material requirements, applicability and
exceptions. Select one main pattern; series recognition (M13) and sound emphasis (M15) can overlay
it.

- For information-led content, try a concrete proof or result, a recognisable problem, a
  comparison, a demonstration or a counterintuitive claim supported by the actual material.
- For action or suspense content, the visible situation can carry the question. Intentional
  silence, no caption and first-person narration are valid choices; do not impose an immediate
  spoken topic or a universal loudness requirement.
- Check the required material before recommending the pattern. A proof-led opening needs owned or
  authorised readable proof; a comparison needs both sides; an action-led opening needs the action.
  A public reference link does not give reuse permission or prove the creator has it.

Write a usable suggestion in the existing mechanism, differentiation, direction or recommendation
field: what appears first, what is said or heard (or why silence is intentional), and what the
combination makes the viewer understand or want to find out. Keep it proportionate to the task;
research does not need a full draft or three mandatory variants.

For example, a caption about saving time can support: "Try showing your own before/after timer
first, then explain the one change." It cannot support: "The video opens with a timer." A watched
action with a clearly visible unresolved outcome can support an action-led recommendation without
requiring a title card or speech.

## Keep method apart from current-task evidence

The patterns are method context, not new viewing observations, calibrated audio measurements,
current account evidence or a popularity ranking. They carry no example videos; never cite one as
proof in this report, and never claim a reference was watched in this run.

Use the patterns' conditions for relevant questions, not a universal ten-item report or pass/fail
score. A trend pattern still needs the workflow's supporting videos; a single opening does not
establish a repeated pattern. Title and cover promises and a closing loop are context only when
they help judge the opening. Advertising thresholds and long-video retention examples are not
universal short-video cutoffs.

Keep metric and missingness audit notes in the workflow's existing structured fields. Give the
creator the supported mechanism and a concrete production choice, in their language, through the
existing renderer. Do not write a second report, alter rankings or add media retrieval to make an
opening claim.
