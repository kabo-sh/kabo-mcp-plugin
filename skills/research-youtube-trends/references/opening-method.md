# Opening mechanisms in trend research

Read this whole file when a provisional Short candidate's mechanism or Creator-fit direction in the
synthesis needs an opening choice. Apply it in the existing mechanism, direction, recommendation,
angle, differentiation or next-shoot fields; it adds no discovery, ranking, watching or model stage.
Preserve the provisional Short classification and the current citation allowlist, and do not apply
short-video timing assumptions to long-form videos.

## Decide what the current evidence supports

Titles, descriptions and untimed transcripts can suggest a topic or writing direction. They cannot
establish the actual first frame, on-screen text timing, first sound, delivery or audiovisual
alignment.

- With titles and descriptions only, keep reference-related findings, including overview and
  pattern labels, about their wording. Label inferred mechanisms as title interpretations ("the
  titles suggest a demonstration direction"), rather than narrating what the creators show or do.
  Reserve action order, visible proof, first frames, text timing, sound and delivery for observed
  media.
- Describe an unobserved opening as a proposed production choice for the creator's next video, not
  a finding about what that video did. For watched evidence, keep picture, heard sound and their
  joint effect distinct; a transcript is not a listening observation.
- Keep current-task citations and metrics unchanged and attached to observed claims. Public
  performance numbers do not establish why an opening worked; do not infer private retention from
  public plays or a reference's success.

## Choose a mechanism that the creator can make

The opening patterns are read only through the script, never from the files under
`references/hook-core/`. `rank` printed the catalog (`HookResearchContext`: each pattern's id, name
and material requirements). For a relevant short-video direction, read one to three chosen patterns
in full (picture, sound, intended joint effect, material requirements, applicability and
exceptions) in this same synthesis:

```bash
cd '<run directory>' && KABO_STATIC_RUN_DIR='<run directory>' python3 '<skill directory>/scripts/prepare_hook_context.py' --platform youtube --purpose trends --pattern-id <Mxx> --output hook-opening-<Mxx>.json
```

Select one main pattern; series recognition (M13) and sound emphasis (M15) can overlay it.

- For information-led content, try a concrete proof or result, a recognisable problem, a
  comparison, a demonstration or a counterintuitive claim supported by the actual material.
- For action or suspense content, the visible situation can carry the question. Intentional
  silence, no caption and first-person narration are valid choices; do not impose an immediate
  spoken topic or a universal loudness requirement. Apply a pattern's checks to the content form.
- Check the required material before recommending the pattern. A proof-led opening needs owned or
  authorised readable proof; a comparison needs both sides; an action-led opening needs the action.
  A public reference link does not give reuse permission or prove the creator has it. Unconfirmed
  materials are preparation requirements, not assets the creator already owns.

Make the recommended opening one complete production choice: what appears first, what is said or
heard (or why silence is intentional), and what the combination makes the viewer understand or want
to find out. Keep it proportionate to the task; research does not need a full draft or three
mandatory variants.

For example, a title about saving time can support: "Try showing your own before/after timer
first, then explain the one change." It cannot support: "The video opens with a timer." A watched
action with a clearly visible unresolved outcome can support an action-led recommendation without
requiring a title card or speech.

## Keep method apart from current-task evidence

The patterns are method context, not new viewing observations, calibrated audio measurements,
current account evidence, private retention or a popularity ranking. They carry no example videos;
never add one to ranked evidence or cite it as proof, and never claim a reference was watched in
this run.

Use the patterns' conditions for relevant questions, not a universal ten-item report or pass/fail
score. A trend pattern still needs the workflow's supporting videos; a single opening does not
establish a repeated pattern. Title and cover promises and a closing loop are context only when
they help judge the opening. Advertising thresholds and long-video retention examples are not
universal short-video cutoffs.

Write the production choice in the delivery language; keep pattern ids, internal labels and helper
commands in task artifacts, outside Creator-facing prose. Keep metric and missingness audit notes
in the existing structured fields. Do not write a second report, alter rankings or add media
retrieval to make an opening claim.
