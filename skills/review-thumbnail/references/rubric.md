# Ten binary checks

Apply these ten editorial dimensions, not a proprietary algorithm or a claimed prediction.
Use only pass/fail, one evidence-grounded sentence and a fix for each fail. Judge originals and
320px previews. No points, totals, ranks by pass count, quality dimension or automatic vetoes.

| ID | Check | Pass anchor | Fail anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| topic_match | Topic Match | The subject clearly connects to the supplied video topic. | Unrelated or unclear topic; without title/summary/source, explain that matching cannot be confirmed. |
| emotion | Emotional Triggers | Expression or scene conveys a clear emotion. | Flat, without a clear emotional trigger. Calm, wonder and anticipation count; a face or exaggerated expression is not required. |
| harmony | Visual Harmony | Colors, type and elements form a coherent whole. | Conflicting elements or a pasted-together appearance. |
| eye_catching | Eye Catching Elements | One focal element catches the eye at small size. | No clear focal point. |
| uncluttered | Uncluttered Composition | Few competing elements and a prominent subject. | Clutter or competing text obscures the subject. |
| color_contrast | Color And Contrast | The subject separates clearly from the background. | Muddy or insufficient contrast; muted palettes can pass when separation is strong. |
| unique_concept | Unique Concept | A specific, distinctive visual idea beyond a generic template. | Interchangeable treatment without a specific idea. Without peer examples, judge only visible conceptual specificity, never claim market-wide originality. |
| execution | Creative Execution | The idea is polished and complete. | Visible roughness, stray fragments, broken lettering, unresolved placeholders or incomplete layout. Legitimate screen UI, source and AI disclosure labels and decoration are not defects merely for existing. |
| curiosity | Curiosity Elicitation | A visible question, tension or incomplete reveal motivates learning more. | The image exhausts its point or lacks a clear question. Do not promise a result absent from the video. |
| resolution_aspect | Resolution & Aspect Ratio | Sharp at intended display size, usable dimensions and the requested ratio. | Blur, insufficient detail or wrong ratio. Use measured pixels and the explicit target; a missing target fails for missing context, not for bad pixels. |

Repeated-review rule: freeze image bytes, context and these anchors. Do not change strictness
because of candidate order or an earlier verdict. Each reason must identify a visible feature or
specific missing evidence. Negative observations should not claim a factual source mismatch unless
source evidence supports it. When a historical defect is outside these ten checks, report that
limitation in evaluation; do not secretly add the old quality framework.

Disclosure labels such as "AI generated", "cloned voice" or "Not financial advice" are compliance
text the creator must keep. Do not count them as clutter, fragments or competing text in harmony,
uncluttered or execution, and never suggest deleting or hiding them. If one covers the subject, a fix
may suggest moving it while keeping it readable and its wording unchanged.

Ground every verdict in that candidate's own observations. Judge each image as if it were alone;
comparisons happen afterwards and never change an item verdict.

Choose the first 2–3 actual failures per candidate (or all failures if fewer). Give practical
changes such as removing a specific competing text block, enlarging the named subject or supplying
the missing topic/format. In comparisons, identify both the preferred candidate's concrete advantage
and the alternative's weakness; equally weak candidates may yield no recommendation.

YouTube long-form thumbnails serve search/recommendations. Short-form covers mainly serve profile
or grid browsing; eye-catching elements and distinctive concepts matter there, without adding
platform weights or claiming observed audience effects.
