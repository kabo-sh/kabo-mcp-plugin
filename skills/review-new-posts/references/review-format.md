# Review format

Write in the creator's language, in plain sentences a busy creator reads in 20 seconds. Lead with
the verdict, then the numbers that support it, then what to do. No tables, no jargon, no
abbreviations they would not use themselves.

## Structure

```markdown
**<Post title, first 8 words> · <1-hour | 24-hour | 7-day> review**

<Verdict: one sentence, with the main number and what it is compared with.>

<Numbers: two or three sentences. Views, likes and comments at this age; engagement rate against
their usual; views per hour since the last check when there was one.>

<What people say (24 hours and 7 days only): the two or three themes in the comments, with one
short quote each, or "No comments yet.">

**Next video**
1. <A concrete suggestion tied to this result.>
2. <A second one, different in kind.>

<Limits, one line, only when review-facts listed any.>
```

## Choosing the comparison

1. `same_age_baseline.multiple` exists (3 or more earlier posts measured at this checkpoint): use
   it. "1,840 views after 24 hours, 1.8 times what your posts usually have at 24 hours."
2. Otherwise use `typical_settled.share_reached`, and say what it is: "In its first hour it reached
   36% of the views your posts usually end up with." Never call this a same-age comparison.
3. For the 7-day review, also compare with `pillar_settled` when it has 3 or more posts: did this
   post do better or worse than others in the same pillar?

Words for the verdict: "well ahead of" (2 times or more), "ahead of" (1.2 to 2), "in line with"
(0.8 to 1.2), "behind" (0.5 to 0.8), "well behind" (under 0.5). Use the number too.

## What each checkpoint is for

- **1 hour:** the opening and the first audience. It is early: say so, and do not call a post a
  failure. A post well behind at 1 hour may still recover. Suggestions here are small and
  immediate: pin a comment that asks a question, reply to early comments, share it to Stories.
- **24 hours:** whether it took off. Comments are now worth reading: questions people ask are the
  best source of the next video. Suggestions: a follow-up that answers the top question, a second
  version of the same idea with a stronger first line.
- **7 days:** the final result for this post. Say whether to make more of this pillar or this angle,
  and what single thing to change next time. Mention a missed checkpoint here if there was one.

## Next-video suggestions

- Tie each one to evidence in this review: a number, a comment theme or the pillar comparison.
- Make it specific enough to film tomorrow: the topic, the first line, what is on screen.
- Stay inside the creator's pillars unless the comments clearly ask for something else; then say
  that it is outside their pillars.
- When the post did well, the first suggestion is usually a follow-up or a part two.
- When it did poorly, change one thing (the opening line, the topic angle or the length), not
  everything, so the next post shows what made the difference.

## Never

- Never state or estimate watch time, retention, reach, saves or shares unless `review-facts`
  returned them.
- Never blame the algorithm, shadowbans or posting time without evidence.
- Never promise a result.
