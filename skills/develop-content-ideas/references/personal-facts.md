# Personal facts and names in creative work

Apply this gate to every publishable surface: title, hook, spoken line, opening frame,
on-screen text, character description, caption and CTA. It applies to a native package
and a creative proposal or draft alike; creative permission does not establish a fact.

- Never invent a creator's years of experience, age, achievements, qualifications or titles.
  Match each claim to evidence about the **same person and the same claim**. A number elsewhere
  in the source, another person's experience, or a vague phrase such as "lifelong" is not proof
  of a specific duration. Do not infer age or tenure from appearance or account history.
- Reuse facts the user explicitly supplied and preserve their source and qualification.
  A creator's self-report stays a self-report, not independently verified proof. Do not turn
  a hypothetical example or an earlier assistant suggestion into a real personal history.
- Without support, remove the specificity or leave a visible placeholder such as `[X years]`,
  `[age]` or `[qualification]` at the affected line. Labeling the whole result "draft" does
  not make an unsupported assertion safe to publish.
- Use a third-party name when the supplied post caption, legible authored on-screen text, explicit user
  confirmation or verified creator statement establishes that person's identity. A name heard
  only through an automatic transcript or automatic on-screen captions is insufficient: use a supported role such as "friend"
  or "coach", or "the other person" when the relationship is unknown. Preserve the raw transcript;
  do not silently correct its spelling or borrow a name from another post. Keep explicitly
  fictional characters distinct from claims about real people.
- Before core validates and renders `idea_set.json`, inspect its text for every personal number, identity claim
  and third-party name. Bind supported claims to the available evidence references/claim ledger;
  remove or replace unsupported specifics. Keep placeholders and qualifications on every repeated
  surface, including the cover and opening. Existing validators check structure, not the truth
  of a biography; a successful validator does not replace this source-to-copy check.

Make any correction in `idea_set.json` before the core call, including an already-permitted
revision; do not add a core pass or a revision. After successful rendering, preserve the exact
`RESULT.md` delivery contract. A late-discovered unsupported claim blocks delivery; it does not
authorize hand-editing the report, rerunning core or presenting the copy as validated.
