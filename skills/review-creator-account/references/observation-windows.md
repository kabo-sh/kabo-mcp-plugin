# Observation windows and public snapshots

Before comparing, distinguish the quantity actually measured:

- A public post count is cumulative at retrieval. Its publication date and a minimum-age cutoff
  do not supply its first 24 hours, seven days, or any other common exposure interval.
- A post-performance comparison needs the same platform, format, metric definition and measured
  duration since each post was published. An account-total or funnel comparison needs equal
  calendar durations, the same cohort/denominator and matching attribution windows. Resolve these
  from the evidence; clarify only missing distinctions. Do not invent historical snapshots.
- Maturity, equal numbers of posts, similar posting dates, or dividing cumulative views by days
  do not establish matched windows. This applies even when every post clears the maturity cutoff.

`scope.metric_comparison` describes the public Analyzer's input: cumulative counts only, no
verified equal windows. Preserve its current counts, ranks and baseline statistics as descriptive
snapshot facts. Say that the ages differ and these are not a fair performance comparison **where
those numbers appear**, not only in a later limitations section. A high count or multiple is not
proof that a topic, format, hook or CTA is stronger. A lower count does not make it weaker. Do not
use either to exclude a reach problem, locate a conversion loss or prescribe copying/stopping a
style. Derive proposed tests from observed wording; label the mechanism as a hypothesis.

For this public input, use `public_observation` or `hypothesis`, not `performance_association`.
The latter is rejected for public snapshot reports even when the cited posts are mature. Do not
simply relabel an unchanged performance claim. Write the bounded patterns first; carry the same
boundary into their headings, the headline, diagnosis and recommendations. A hypothesis may name
what to test, but cannot present an unmeasured association as its evidence.

If the user supplies genuinely aligned measurements, answer the supported comparison from those
figures and explicitly name their source, metric and windows. Do not insert them into the public
snapshot or imply that its report validated them. Equal declared windows need no extra question.
For sales/funnel figures, follow [sales-evidence.md](sales-evidence.md). Lack of private outcomes
must not suppress a useful description of observed content or a concrete next test.
